# Changelog

User-facing changes, newest first. Dates are in Asia/Colombo time.

## Unreleased

### Sprint 1: Accounts and friends

- Sign in with Google, Facebook or an email link. Links expire after 15 minutes and work once; an expired link explains itself and offers a new one.
- Short onboarding: name, username (checked as you type), country and default currency (LKR for Sri Lanka).
- Edit your profile and photo. Photos are cropped and shrunk on the phone before upload.
- Link more sign-in methods. Signing in with a new provider whose email matches your account asks you to confirm first.
- Find friends by exact username or email (emails are never shown), send requests, accept or decline (declining is silent), cancel requests.
- Personal invite link: share it, and anyone who opens it can add you in one tap, even if they sign up first. Create a new link at any time.
- Friends list sorted by name, with search and shared trip counts. Remove a friend with confirmation; shared trips are not affected.

### Sprint 0: Foundation

- Installable app shell with a bottom navigation, light and dark themes, designed for 360 px screens.
- Landing page.
